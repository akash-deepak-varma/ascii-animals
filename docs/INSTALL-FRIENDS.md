# ASCII Animals New Tab: install and test (about 5 minutes)

Thanks for trying this! It turns your New Tab page into a live scene of ASCII animals
(butterflies, koi, jellyfish, dragonflies, frogs, cranes, crabs) with a glass search box.
It works in **Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and other Chromium browsers**. Not Firefox or Safari.

This is a test build, so you install it by hand. Nothing is sent anywhere: see PRIVACY.md.

## Install

1. **Download** `ascii-animals-newtab-v0.9.0.zip` and **extract** it
   (Windows: right-click, *Extract All*. Mac: double-click).
2. **Move the extracted folder somewhere permanent** (for example Documents). The browser reads the
   extension from that folder, so if you delete it, the extension stops working.
3. Open `chrome://extensions` (Edge: `edge://extensions`) and turn on **Developer mode**
   (a switch at the top right, or in the left menu on Edge).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the folder that contains `manifest.json`
   (the one you extracted, not a folder inside it).
5. Open a new tab. If the browser asks whether to keep the new tab page, keep it.

Your browser may show a "disable developer mode extensions" notice when it starts. That is normal for
hand-installed extensions: dismiss it.

## Update to a newer build

Extract the new ZIP **over the old folder** (replace the files), then on the extensions page click the
circular **reload** arrow on the ASCII Animals card.

## Remove it

Extensions page, then **Remove**. (Please do this before installing the Web Store version later, otherwise
two extensions fight over the new tab.)

## What I would love you to try

Tick off what you can, and tell me what felt wrong, slow, ugly or confusing.

- [ ] Click each of the 7 animals in the top bar. Do they all look good and move well?
- [ ] Click on the background: they should react. On **Koi**, a click drops food and nearby fish swim over.
- [ ] Type a search and press Enter (should use your normal search engine). Type an address like `example.com`.
- [ ] Open **Tweaks** (top right) and drag a few sliders. Close and reopen the tab: is your choice remembered?
- [ ] Open **Settings** (the gear). Try: your name in the greeting, hiding the greeting, **Power saver**.
- [ ] **Shortcuts**: choose *Bookmarks and folders* (or *Top sites*). Your browser should ask permission first, once, for bookmarks and site icons together.
  - [ ] Do your bookmarks show with the right site icons? Do your **folders** show, and open a menu with what is inside (including folders inside folders)?
  - [ ] If you have more bookmarks than fit, is there a **More** tile that lists the rest?
  - [ ] Add, rename or delete a bookmark in another tab: does the row update without a reload?
  - [ ] If you say **no** to the prompt, does an *Allow bookmarks* tile appear, and does it work when you click it?
- [ ] **Auto-rotate**: try *Each new tab*.
- [ ] **Sync across devices** (only if you are signed in to the browser).
- [ ] Make the window very small and very large.
- [ ] On a laptop on battery: do the fans spin up or the battery drain? Does Power saver help?
